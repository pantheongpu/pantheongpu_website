# Pantheon Code of Conduct

We want Pantheon to be a respectful, technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and assume good intent.
- Discuss hardware results and methods with evidence, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy: do not post credentials, personal information, or private infrastructure details.
- Follow vendor, platform, and local safety guidance when stress testing hardware.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Encouraging unsafe electrical, thermal, or hardware modifications without clear risk disclosure.
- Spam, misleading benchmark claims, or intentionally falsified submissions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, hide, or remove content that violates these rules. Repeated or severe violations may result in participation restrictions. To report a concern privately, use a GitHub private security advisory.
